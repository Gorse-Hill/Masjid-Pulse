Masjid Pulse

A free prayer-time display for your mosque TV. Accurate, readable from the back of the hall, works offline, and is easy for any volunteer to update from a phone.

Register your mosque Admin login Open TV display

Set up in three steps

  1. Register your mosque and enter today's adhan and iqamah times.
  2. Open /tv/ on the TV (or install the Android TV app). It shows a 5-digit code.
  3. In the admin, go to Devices, type the code, then press Publish.

Install after Maghrib, connect before Isha, trust it before Fajr.
